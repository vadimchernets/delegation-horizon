# Consent form: pilot of a measure of work handed to AI agents

This form is only for this study. It does not extend, replace or reuse any other consent you have
given, including for any course or programme.

**What the study is.** We test a way of measuring how large a piece of work a person can hand to AI
agents and have confirmed by an independent check. You will work on tasks in {DOMAIN} over about six
months, using the AI tools we provide, and log each task you hand over.

**What we record.** For each task: dates, the task's size in reference hours, your minutes of
attention, which kind of check was used and whether it passed, and the fields listed in the study's
log format. We record the content of your work only for tasks from our task bank, so that checkers can
review them. We do not record the content of your own work unless you choose to log it, and you can
log your own work without its content.

**What we do not do.** We do not use your name in any data; you get a label such as P07. We do not
share data with your employer or school. The measure is not a test of you as a person and is not used
for any decision about you.

**Time.** About {HOURS} hours per week for two periods of 91 days, plus two short sessions.

until {DATE}; after that, pseudonymous data may already be in published analyses.

CC BY 4.0 without any field that could identify you.
