# Scope statement: what a rung means and does not mean

*Paper: Sections 12 and 13 (what the evidence does not show yet; scope).*
*For: anyone who prints a rung in a posting, a certificate or a profile. Attach this text beside it.*

> **Verified delegation horizon: [Domain] . [Rung] . [Gain step] . [Quarter]**
>
> **What it means.** In the stated domain and quarter, this person handed pieces of work of at least
> the stated size (in a qualified specialist's by-hand hours) to AI agents, and at least eight in ten of
> them passed a check the agents could not see. The gain step is verified work per hour of the person's
> attention in the last 91 days. Failed and abandoned pieces are counted. The check types
> were: [hidden tests / qualified reviewer / real use / other model family with sources].
>
> **What it does not mean.** It is not a rating of intelligence, of skill without agents, or of
> suitability for other domains. It does not say the person could do the work by hand. It does not
> describe a different checking regime, or a later date: rungs are re-verified each quarter.
>
> **How to check it.** Ask for the work log and the acceptance cards behind it. The log can be scored
> with the open scorer at github.com/vadimchernets/delegation-horizon (`horizon.py` or `checker.html`).
>
> **Status.** The rung thresholds are provisional, tested on worked examples and awaiting the human
> pilot. Use the record as one input among several.
