# F8. Token counts as an official evaluation criterion (DRAFT)

Archives: Factiva or ProQuest business news, Google News, company newsroom pages. Window: 1 January to 31 December 2027.
Queries (each with the company name of every Fortune 500 company of the 2027 list):
- "token" AND ("performance review" OR "evaluation" OR "leaderboard") AND ("AI" OR "Claude" OR "Copilot" OR "ChatGPT")
- "tokenmaxxing"
- "AI usage" AND "performance review" AND ("tokens" OR "token count")
Counts only when the employer documents it, or two independent outlets report it as an official criterion. Surveys about AI use do not count.
