## What you will do
[Three to five lines of real work in the domain.]

## What we look for
- Experience in [domain]: you know what good work looks like here and can spot a plausible error.
- Evidence that you deliver work through AI agents and show it is right: for example a record such as
  "[domain] . Day . x5 . 2026-Q4" with its log, a portfolio of accepted work with the checks it passed,
  or our paid one-day work test.
- Judgement about what must never be left unchecked.

## What we do not require
- A degree. If you have one, tell us; if you do not, the work test carries the same weight.
- Programming employment. If you can write code, it helps; it is not the test.

## How we select
A paid one-day work test in the domain, scored by a reviewer who has not seen your CV.
